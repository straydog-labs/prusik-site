Why this is different

The Method

01 — Technique

Cleaner movement, on purpose.

We isolate the inefficient patterns — feet, hips, sequence, clip — and rebuild them so the hard moves cost less.

02 — Video

Watch the attempt, not the story of it.

Short clips of the work, reviewed together. You see the missed foot, the rushed clip, the extra move. Then we go back and do it again.

03 — Mental game

Attention is a skill. Train it.

Breath, arousal, and self-talk get coached the same way movement does — in the session, not as a pep talk after.
