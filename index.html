Eighteen years coaching athletes. Eight years aimed at the rock.

The Method

Fear is a skill.
Train it like one.

01

Reset before the redline

A short, repeatable sequence — stop, breathe, reset — used the moment heart rate spikes, so the climber chooses the next move instead of reacting to fear.

02

Allowed to learn

Sessions are framed around learning the move, not flashing it. Removing the pressure to send on the first try is what actually keeps climbers from tipping into panic.

03

Technique under pressure

Clipping, footwork, and route-reading are trained specifically at the moments they tend to fall apart — not in isolation, away from the stress that breaks them.

04

A plan that holds you accountable

Monthly goals, a set cadence, and a coach who proposes the structure instead of asking a climber to design their own accountability from scratch.
